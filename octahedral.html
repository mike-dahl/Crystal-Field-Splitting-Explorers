Teaching note: This is a qualitative visualization of the electrostatic crystal field model. Orbital colors and glow are illustrative, not physical changes in electron clouds. The t2g set decreases by 0.4Δo and the eg set increases by 0.6Δo relative to the barycenter. Actual splitting depends on metal, oxidation state, ligand identity and geometry, not distance alone.
